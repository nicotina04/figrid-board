# figrid-board 0.8.2 state-update optimizations

Date: 2026-07-25 (Asia/Seoul)

Baseline: figrid-board 0.8.1,
`cdfe13af6842b0def8a09da57d4c3e7f26c535f1`.

## Shipped changes

### Packed Pattern4 windows

The pbrain now maintains each 11-cell Pattern4 window as a packed 22-bit
value. A make or undo changes one two-bit slot in each affected window and
then performs the existing mapped-id lookup, instead of rereading all 11
cells.

- hot make+undo update: about 15.3x faster;
- fixed-depth product wall ratio versus 0.8.1: `0.78885`;
- wall saving: `21.11%`;
- one-sided 95% game-block bootstrap upper: `0.79932`;
- 2-second actual-visited NPS ratio: `1.2796`;
- 30-second actual-visited NPS ratio: `1.2957`;
- extra active search-sidecar state: 3,600 bytes;
- one-time root enable: about 16 microseconds.

The original campaign also completed a 4,000-game internal final and
500-paired-opening Pela transfer:

- internal score: 2,146.5/4,000 = 53.6625%;
- one-sided 95% Wilson lower: 52.3636%;
- exact decisive-game p-value: `3.7623e-6`;
- Pela: 36.6% baseline versus 37.0% packed;
- paired bootstrap lower: -1.2 percentage points, above the registered
  -2.0-point non-inferiority margin;
- protocol errors: 0.

### Exact-order candidate frontier

After root VCT fails, the main alpha-beta search maintains the radius-2
candidate frontier incrementally. Buckets keyed by the minimum occupied
source reproduce the legacy move order exactly. Root VCT remains on the
legacy path so it pays no frontier-maintenance cost.

Measured incrementally on top of packed windows:

| Workload | B/A result | 95% upper | Nodes B/A | NPS B/A | Depth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| VCT OFF, fixed depth 4, 1,022 roots | wall `0.96815` | `0.97500` | `1.00000` | `1.03298` | exact |
| Product VCT ON, fixed depth 4, 1,022 roots | wall `0.98685` | `0.99157` | `1.00000` | `1.01336` | exact |
| 2-second class, 16 positions | wall `0.99829` | descriptive | `1.00216` | `1.00389` | p50 9 -> 9 |
| 30-second class, 4 positions | wall `0.99956` | descriptive | `1.03283` | `1.03329` | p50 12 -> 12 |

The fixed-depth product comparison had zero best-move, result, depth, or node
mismatches. The timed comparisons intentionally differ in completed work at
the deadline. The first product B1 arm had one root-VCT proof finish on the
150 ms deadline boundary before A3 could be enabled; that raw arm was retained
outside the crate and rerun once. The rerun restored zero fixed-depth
mismatches and is the checked-in summary.

Same-binary 30-game sanity, two seconds per move with alternating sides:

- A2-only: 13 wins;
- A2+A3: 17 wins;
- draws/errors: 0/0.

The frontier occupies about 7,744 bytes while active. Together with packed
windows, peak optional search-sidecar state is about 11,344 bytes. The
frontier is enabled only around the main search and dropped afterward.

## Combined interpretation

The independently registered fixed-depth ratios compound to:

```text
0.78885 * 0.9868546 = 0.77848
```

This indicates approximately 22.15% less fixed-depth wall time than 0.8.1.
It is a composition of two same-policy A/B measurements, not a single direct
baseline-to-final estimate. Similarly, the observed time-control NPS ratios
compound to about 1.285x at two seconds and 1.339x at 30 seconds.

No evaluator weights, feature mapping, VCT proof rules, or move-ordering
policy changed.

## Runtime policy and rollback

The public `Board` keeps the exact 0.8.1 ten-field layout. Both accelerators
live in a `Searcher`-owned sidecar and remain OFF for ordinary library
callers; the shipped `pbrain-figrid` enables both by default. An integration
test compiles an exhaustive 0.8.1-style `Board` struct literal to guard patch
version source compatibility.

- `NORU_PACKED_LINE_WINDOWS=off`: restore the 0.8.1 Pattern4 updater;
- `NORU_CANDIDATE_FRONTIER=off`: retain packed windows but restore legacy
  candidate generation.

The environment switches control the shipped pbrain; library callers can opt
in through `Searcher::set_use_packed_line_windows` and
`Searcher::set_use_candidate_frontier`.
